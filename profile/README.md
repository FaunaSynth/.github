# 🦉 FaunaSynth

### AI for Wildlife Monitoring & Conservation

**FaunaSynth** is a research initiative advancing wildlife monitoring and conservation through AI, synthetic data, multimodal learning, and computational ecology.

Our goal is to develop **adaptable technologies** for:

- 🐾 **Species recognition** across images, video, and audio
- 🧠 **Understanding animal behavior** from multimodal signals
- 🚨 **Detecting threats** such as poaching, habitat disturbance, and illegal activity
- 🌍 **Supporting evidence-based conservation** worldwide

Conservation data is often scarce, imbalanced, and costly to label, especially for rare species and remote habitats. FaunaSynth addresses this by combining synthetic data generation with multimodal models that generalize across regions, sensors, and ecosystems.
